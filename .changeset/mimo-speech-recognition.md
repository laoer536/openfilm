Xiaomi MiMo writes down what is said, for subtitles, with your own key: connect one in Studio (the OpenFilm logo at the top left → Providers) or set `MIMO_API_KEY`, then choose Xiaomi MiMo for transcripts or run `openfilm get asr --via mimo --src assets/talk.mp4`. Its speech recognition times no words: the transcript's lines spread over the recording, and Studio cuts them into subtitles as it does any other.
